# Aura SEO plan

Prepared: 2026-11-28  
Goal: Generate relevant local enquiries and appointments for Aura in Matara, rather than general skincare traffic.

## Strategy and sources

Adapted from [The Complete SEO & AI SEO Course for 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7DRO4rEIHDk), using an [available transcript](https://viewrankai.com/tools/youtube-transcript/7DRO4rEIHDk) because the YouTube page did not load in the research tools. The approach is to understand search intent, prioritise service pages that generate enquiries, then build supporting content and relevant mentions. Local visibility is an additional priority for this clinic.

This is a proposed plan, not a record of completed SEO work. Recheck implementation status before starting each task.

## Week 1: Foundation and measurement

Code audit at the time of planning:

- Home and Services lack their own SEO metadata.
- About and Gallery are empty. (About is being implemented after this plan was requested.)
- No sitemap, robots file, canonical URLs or structured data were found.
- No analytics or enquiry tracking was found.

Tasks:

- [ ] Confirm the production domain and verify a production build.
- [ ] Add unique titles and descriptions, canonical URLs and social-sharing previews.
- [ ] Create sitemap.xml and robots.txt, excluding unfinished pages from the sitemap. Remove unfinished routes from navigation or apply an appropriate noindex policy until complete.
- [ ] Set up Google Search Console and analytics.
- [ ] Track Call, WhatsApp and Directions clicks separately. These indicate interest, not confirmed appointments.
- [ ] Check mobile performance, image loading, broken links and indexing.
- [ ] Add accurate clinic structured data using only confirmed facts; validate using Google's tools.

Reference: [Google local-business structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business).

## Weeks 1–2: Local visibility

- [ ] Claim or review the Google Business Profile.
- [ ] Confirm business name, full address, map pin, phone, hours and appointment arrangements.
- [ ] Select appropriate categories and list available services.
- [ ] Add genuine clinic photographs and the website URL.
- [ ] Keep information consistent across the website and social profiles.
- [ ] Request honest reviews without incentives or filtering.
- [ ] Respond to reviews without disclosing patient information.

Local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence: [Google's local-ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091).

## Week 2: Research and improve enquiry-generating pages

These are candidate keyword themes, not validated search-volume or competition findings:

| Page | Candidate search intent |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Skin clinic in Matara; family healthcare in Matara |
| Skin care | Acne treatment in Matara; pigmentation treatment in Matara |
| Hair care | Hair-fall treatment in Matara |
| Body care | Body-contouring services in Matara |
| Aesthetics | Facials, waxing and grooming in Matara |
| Contact | Aura location, phone number and appointments |

- [ ] Validate candidates against Sri Lankan search results and available keyword data.
- [ ] Choose priorities using actual patient demand, clinic priorities and search intent.
- [ ] Avoid dermatologist/specialist terms unless verified qualifications support them.
- [ ] Retain /services as the overview.
- [ ] Create dedicated pages for the highest-priority categories or treatments rather than thin pages for every service.
- [ ] Keep existing accordions where useful. Accordion anchors do not create separate pages.
- [ ] Link relevant pages to appointment/contact options and to related services.

## Weeks 2–3: Credible clinic-specific content

- [ ] Complete About with verified qualifications, registration, experience and the clinic's approach.
- [ ] Have Dr. Samantha review medical descriptions before publication.
- [ ] Describe realistic expectations and suitability where relevant.
- [ ] Replace generic claims with accurate details of services actually available at Aura.
- [ ] Keep AI illustrations clearly labelled. They are not evidence of treatment results.
- [ ] Prefer genuine clinic/team photos where available and appropriately authorised.

Reference: [Google's helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).

## Weeks 3–4: One supporting topic cluster

- [ ] Choose one priority topic, such as acne care, after its service page is ready.
- [ ] Publish a small set of doctor-reviewed answers to common patient questions.
- [ ] Link these articles to the relevant service page and contact options.
- [ ] Build one useful cluster before expanding into unrelated subjects.
- [ ] Seek legitimate mentions from relevant local organisations and professional connections.
- [ ] Avoid purchased backlink packages and mass directory submissions.

## Monthly: Measure and improve

Review indexed pages, search queries, local impressions/clicks, contact-action clicks, and confirmed enquiries/appointments where the clinic can record them. Investigate pages that attract visitors but few enquiries. Compare trends against an initial baseline rather than promising rankings or results within a fixed period.

Write clear answers for ordinary search visitors and AI-search users. Google does not require special AI SEO markup, and inclusion is not guaranteed: [Google AI-search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

## First implementation batch

Metadata, sitemap/robots, accurate clinic details, measurement and About.

Required inputs: production domain; confirmed address and hours; verified doctor qualifications/registration; genuine clinic photos; appropriate access to Google Business Profile and Search Console.
